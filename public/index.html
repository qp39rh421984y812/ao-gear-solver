How targets work

Fill either box, or both. At least is a floor the solver pushes up to; hard cap is a ceiling it will not cross — caps outrank floors, so if the two can't both hold, the cap wins and the shortfall is reported. Putting the same number in both pins the stat, which the solver must then land on exactly — use it sparingly. For a stat you simply want plenty of, fill only at least; a cap of 0 is the way to say "none of this at all".

Type - in the at-least box to shun a stat: the solver strips it out wherever it can, and only accepts it where a rule leaves no alternative. A hard cap of 0 is the strict version.

Insanity and drawback cost EP, so they are driven to zero unless you ask for them. Insanity only comes from Atlantean Essence, at +1 per slot, so whole numbers up to one per gear slot are reachable; drawback comes from Painite gems and a few armour pieces in steps of 0.5.

Stat priority

How priority works

Targets are satisfied first; priority then decides where any leftover budget goes. Set them in the order you want the spare stats spent — highest gets it first, off means don't spend there at all. It also decides which target survives when two can't both be met.

The scale is per point of efficiency spent, not per stat point: defense at ×5 still buys far more defense points than power at ×8 buys power, because defense is cheap. Big gaps between ranks make the order strict.

How insanity is spent

Insanity comes only from Atlantean Essence, at +1 per slot, and it pays out into the first stat the piece is missing. Put it on a piece with no power of its own — Sunken and dragon chest and leg pieces — and that payout is +12 power at level 175. The default refuses insanity anywhere it would land on haste, pierce, resistance or regeneration instead.

How attempts work

Each attempt starts from scratch, so a tight build that only lands once in ten tries will still be found — the best result across all attempts is the one you get. You can stop early at any point and keep the best so far.

Matching rules

Comma-separated, matched anywhere in the name — items, gems, enchants and modifiers alike. End one with a dragon colour to block just that version: grand dragoneye amulet black rules out the black one and leaves every other colour available.

EP (Efficiency Points) weighs every stat on one scale: power ÷ 0.3, defense ÷ 3, each substat at face value, and insanity / drawback as penalties that grow with level. Higher EP means less wasted stat budget — so among all gear sets that hit your targets, this returns the most efficient one.
