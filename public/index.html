Magic & fighting-style gear

Arcanium, Arcmancer and Arcsphere pieces come in one version per magic, and Fighting gear in one per style. Your class decides how many you carry (a Mage two magics, a Berserker two styles, a Warrior none). Leave a box on Solver picks and it chooses whichever magic or style gives the best gear; pick one to lock it in. Whatever the build uses is written into the build code, so the gear builder keeps every magic- and style-specific piece exactly as solved.

Spirit points scale Spirit armour (Oracle / Alitheia gear) the same way the builder does, so put in your real allocation.

How targets work

Fill either box, or both. At least is a floor the solver pushes up to; hard cap is a ceiling it will not cross. Caps outrank floors: if both can't hold, the cap wins and the shortfall is reported. The same number in both boxes pins the stat exactly. For a stat you simply want plenty of, fill only at least; a cap of 0 means "none of this at all".

Type - in the at-least box to shun a stat: it is stripped wherever possible. If no gear set can meet every rule, you get the one that misses by the least.

Insanity (from Atlantean, +1 per piece, capped at 5) and drawback (Painite, a few armour pieces) cost EP, so they stay at zero unless a rule needs them. Warding counts only up to 6.

Stat priority

How priority works

Targets are satisfied first; priority then decides where spare budget goes, and which floor survives when two can't both be met. Highest gets it first, off means don't spend there at all.

The scale is per point of efficiency, not per stat point: defense at ×5 still buys far more defense points than power at ×8 buys power, because defense is cheap.

How Atlantean pays out

Atlantean adds +1 insanity and a bonus to the first of power, defense, size, dexterity, range, haste that the piece has none of. If the piece and its jewels already have all six, the bonus falls back to power (+12 at level 170+). The solver uses this: jewels that cover all six stats turn Atlantean into a power modifier.

How attempts work

Each attempt is an independent search from its own starting points; the best across all of them is kept. Attempts run in parallel on your CPU cores. Stop at any time and keep the best so far.

Set an at least floor, a hard cap, or both on any stat, then press Find build. Leave a row blank if you don't care — it is still raised when it's free EP. Type - to shun a stat, and use the priority sliders to say which stats matter most.

Your gear set will appear here.

EP (Efficiency Points) weighs every stat on one scale, exactly as the gear builder does: power ÷ 0.3, defense ÷ 3, each substat at face value, warding as a bonus and insanity / drawback as penalties that grow with level. Among all gear sets that meet your rules, the solver returns the highest-EP one (re-weighted by your priorities).

Item data is read live from the AO Gear Builder when it can be, so new items appear here as soon as they appear there. Every stat formula — item scaling, enchants, modifiers, Atlantean, Imbued, faction modifiers, jewel fusion and tiers, spirit scaling, warding and insanity caps — is ported from the builder and checked against it build-for-build.
